The forged answer

Champion plus the grafts that survived critique.

Plan-tiered quotas, enforced by a Redis token bucket per API key, with soft limits for paid plans. Paid keys get warning headers at 80% and a 24 hour grace period before any 429. Free keys are hard-limited.
